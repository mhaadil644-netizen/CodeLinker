💎 Luxury Perfume Bottle Redesign Prompt — MED Parfum

Create a luxury perfume bottle design for the brand MED Parfum, inspired by Mediterranean elegance and timeless sophistication.
The bottle should be made of transparent or tinted glass with premium reflections, giving a feeling of purity and exclusivity.
It should have smooth, refined curves — elegant yet modern.
Add subtle gold or silver accents around the neck or cap, and a heavy glass base that reflects light beautifully.
The logo “MED Parfum” should appear embossed or engraved in minimalist lettering.

Style inspiration: Dior, Maison Francis Kurkdjian, Louis Vuitton fragrances.

Details to include:

Material: High-quality glass with light diffusion and gradient color (e.g., soft amber, deep blue, crystal clear).

Shape: Unique but balanced — slightly rounded rectangular bottle or cylindrical with faceted edges.

Cap: Magnetic metallic cap (gold, silver, or matte black finish).

Vibe: Mediterranean luxury — clean, sensual, and timeless.

Lighting: soft studio lighting, luxury product photography style.

Background: neutral or marble texture, to highlight the bottle.

Make it look realistic, premium, and photogenic, perfect for a luxury perfume e-commerce website.

🌿 Example Variants You Can Ask For:

“A luxury glass perfume bottle with emerald green tint and gold accents — inspired by the Mediterranean sea.”

“Modern perfume bottle made of thick glass with a silver magnetic cap and engraved logo — elegant, unisex design.”

“Square glass perfume bottle with rounded edges, deep amber gradient, and a minimalist golden logo MED Parfum.”

“Crystal perfume bottle design with sculpted sides like a diamond — luxurious and photorealistic.”

📸 Tips for perfect AI generation:

Always add “ultra realistic, 8K, product photo, studio lighting” at the end of your prompt.

Try 3–4 variations to find the best shape and vibe.

Once you find the design you love → you can re-use it for packaging (box design, label, etc.).
